Who We Are

A house devoted to
what endures

Founded in the United Arab Emirates, House of Living Heritage is a dedicated conservation practice at the intersection of craftsmanship, scholarship, and cultural stewardship. We work alongside government bodies, private estates, and community custodians to protect the physical and intangible heritage that defines our region.

From wind-tower architecture in the coastal towns to oral traditions passed through generations, we believe every thread of heritage deserves the same reverence — and the same meticulous care.

Conservation Work

Preservation with
purpose and precision

Our conservation practice spans the full spectrum of heritage care — from emergency stabilisation to long-term stewardship programmes rooted in international best practice and local knowledge.

01

Architectural Conservation

Structural assessment, material analysis, and sensitive restoration of historic buildings — coral stone dwellings, forts, mosques, and traditional courtyard houses across the Emirates.

02

Material & Artefact Care

Expert treatment of textiles, manuscripts, ceramics, and decorative arts. Climate-controlled documentation, cleaning, and preventive conservation for museum and private collections.

03

Intangible Heritage

Recording oral histories, safeguarding craft traditions — from sadu weaving to pearling songs — and developing community-led programmes that keep living culture vibrant.

04

Heritage Assessment

Comprehensive surveys, significance evaluations, and conservation management plans aligned with UNESCO frameworks and UAE national heritage guidelines.

05

Adaptive Reuse

Thoughtful transformation of heritage structures for contemporary use — honouring original fabric while ensuring viability for future generations.

06

Capacity Building

Training programmes for conservators, artisans, and heritage professionals — cultivating local expertise to sustain our conservation mission long into the future.

Our Approach

Reverence before
intervention

Every project begins with listening — to the building, to the community, to the land. We conduct thorough research before a single tool is lifted, ensuring that each intervention is reversible, documented, and true to the spirit of the original.

  • Research & Documentation Archival study, photographic surveys, and condition mapping
  • Minimal Intervention Preserving authentic fabric; repairing only what time has claimed
  • Local Craftsmanship Partnering with master artisans who carry generational knowledge
  • Long-term Stewardship Maintenance plans that protect our work for decades to come
